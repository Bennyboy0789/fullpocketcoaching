Culture by Design, Not Default.

Lead Boldly. Adapt Quickly. Thrive Collectively.

  • 90 Minutes

Summary

In this course, Marni Blythe, Fractional COO and author of Culture Catalyst, reveals how intentional culture design can transform heathcare organizations from the inside out. Participants will learn how to strengthen leadership, accountability, and communication to create a thriving team culture that drives innovation, patient satisfaction, and sustainable growth.

Target Outcome

Equip healthcare leaders to confidently lead change, build a high-performance culture, and embed continuous innovation into daily operations—resulting in measurable improvements in team engagement, patient satisfaction, and profitability.

Format

Interactive virtual or in-person workshop (ideal for leadership teams & high-potential emerging leaders in independent dental practices)

Learning Objectives

Evaluate their current practice culture using a guided diagnostic tool to identify areas of strength and opportunities for growth.

Recognize how daily behaviors shape culture and how small, intentional shifts can create significant impact on team performance and morale.

Identify and reframe one culture-limiting behavior within their team and develop a corresponding growth behavior to drive positive change.

Apply leadership strategies that foster trust, adaptability, and accountability within the dental team.

Commit to one actionable change that aligns with their core values and contributes to a healthier, more innovative practice culture.

Practice Culture Snapshot

Team Cohesion

the formula for predictable growth

The Formula for Predictable Growth

  • 90 Minutes - 2 Hours

Summary

Most healthcare owners tracking numbers—but few are leading through them. In this 90-minute interactive workshop, Marni Blythe— Fractional COO, and author of Culture Catalyst—guides dental owners through a leadership-centered approach to production, growth, and goal achievement. This session reframes numbers as a leadership tool, not a source of stress—helping owners build practices that are profitable, resilient, and aligned.

Target Outcome

Equip healthcare owners to confidently lead their practice using clear metrics, aligned goals, and emotionally intelligent leadership—resulting in stronger production, healthier teams, and sustainable growth without chaos.

Format

Interactive workshop (ideal for entire dental teams—clinical and administrative)

Learning Objectives

Identify the 5–7 critical metrics that truly drive production, collections, and capacity—moving beyond surface-level P&Ls.

Apply a weekly scorecard to create clarity, traction, and accountability across the leadership team.

Translate practice goals into executable priorities (Rocks) that align doctors, managers, and teams around shared outcomes.

Recognize how leadership behaviors and culture directly impact production, efficiency, and patient flow.

Lead through hardship and pressure using emotionally intelligent decision-making rather than reactive or fear-based management.

navigating high-stakes conversations

Communicating with Clarity, Confidence & Compassion in High-Stakes Moments

  • 75 Minutes

Summary

Based on the CORE™ Framework, this workshop helps healthcare professionals navigate high-stakes conversations with clarity and care. From giving feedback to handling performance issues, participants will learn how to manage their emotions, avoid common pitfalls, and address tough topics with courage and compassion.

Target Outcome

Equip healthcare professionals with practical tools to navigate high-stakes, emotionally charged conversations—transforming conflict into connection and clarity.

Format

Live keynote-style interactive workshop (ideal for practice owners, clinical leads, office managers, and emerging leaders)

Learning Objectives

Define what constitutes a “critical conversation” and recognize the top 3 types most often avoided in dental practices.

Apply the CORE Framework to confidently structure and lead a crucial conversation that minimizes defensiveness and maximizes clarity.

Identify at least one of their personal emotional triggers and use a grounding strategy to manage reactivity in real-time.

Distinguish when to avoid, confront, or postpone conflict—and explain why timing is critical to successful resolution.

Demonstrate the use of compassion-driven language (e.g., “Can you help me understand…”) to create psychological safety during tough discussions.
