PROGRAMS

Culture by design. not by default

In this course, Marni Blythe, Fractional COO and author of Culture Catalyst, reveals how intentional culture design can transform organizations from the inside out. Participants will learn how to strengthen leadership, accountability, and communication to create a thriving team culture that drives innovation, patient satisfaction, and sustainable growth.

Human Intelligence in an AI World

This keynote or workshop gives participants strategies to build resilience, emotional control, and mental focus. Participants learn how to stay calm under pressure, communicate with confidence, and lead themselves and their teams with strength and intention, even on the hardest days.

the formula for predictable growth

Most organizations are tracking numbers, but few are leading through them. In this 90-minute interactive workshop, Marni guides participants through a leadership-centered approach to production, growth, and goal achievement. This session reframes numbers as a leadership tool, not a source of stress, helping owners build practices that are profitable, resilient, and aligned.

Navigating high stakes conversations

Based on the CORE™ Framework, this workshop helps professionals navigate high-stakes conversations with clarity and care. From giving feedback to handling performance issues, participants will learn how to manage their emotions, avoid common pitfalls, and address tough topics with courage and compassion.

Most leadership challenges in don’t come from a lack of effort. They come from unspoken tension, misaligned expectations, and systems that no longer match the reality of the practice. 

Marni Blythe works with organizations that want high-performing leadership and a culture that improves retention, performance, and profitability.

As a Professional Speaker, Fractional COO/CMO/Integrator and leadership coach, Marni brings real-world conversations about leadership, culture, communication, and operations to the forefront through keynotes, workshops, and breakout sessions.

Her approach is simple: people don’t need more theory. They need practical tools, honest conversations, and strategies they can implement immediately.

Drawing from years inside independent practices, Marni helps teams:

  • Improve communication and accountability
  • Strengthen leadership and emotional intelligence
  • Address the issues quietly blocking growth
  • Create healthier, more aligned workplace cultures

Every program is designed to create meaningful reflection, practical action, and lasting momentum that positively impacts teams, patients, and culture long after the session ends.
