Your Leaders Shape Your Culture. Your Culture Shapes Everything.

Creating Psychological Safety

We’ve spent countless hours in the room with CEOs and leadership teams, so we know what you’re actually dealing with. Long days. Constant fires. Priorities competing for the same five minutes. Wearing way too many hats just to keep things moving.

Maybe you’re frustrated. Overwhelmed. Wondering why you’re putting in so much effort and not seeing the results to show for it.

That’s the reality Marni Blythe brings to the stage.

Through her keynotes, interactive programs, and the Full Pocket Method™, she gives leaders tools they can actually use. Tools to create clarity, build accountability, sharpen communication, and turn a vision into something that actually happens day to day, not just on paper.

Her approach comes from years of helping leaders work through the operational and human stuff that actually shapes performance, culture, and growth that sticks. Audiences don’t just leave feeling inspired. They leave with frameworks they can use the next morning to lead with more confidence, build stronger teams, and get results that last.

The Full Pocket Method™ simplifies growth through a clear and actionable framework focused on:

  • Mindset – developing emotionally intelligent, resilient leadership
  • Tools – building systems and processes that create consistency and control
  • Execution – turning vision into action with accountability and structure
  • Evaluation – measuring progress and course-correcting with clarity
  • Traction – creating sustainable momentum that keeps your business growing

It’s time to stop spinning your wheels and start leading with intention, balance, and confidence — so your business works for you instead of the other way around.

What I Do

I work with corporate executives, professionals, practice owners, and entrepreneurial leaders who are done being stuck.

I’ve seen teams stuck in survival mode because nobody’s dealt with the real bottleneck or the burnout everyone’s pretending isn’t there.

As a speaker, I run keynotes, breakout sessions, and hands-on workshops that go past a good feeling that fades by Monday. My programs build what I call Human Intelligence.

The distinctly human stuff leaders need so they can actually think clearly when things get hard, not just react.

That means real strategy around leadership, culture, operations, communication, emotional intelligence.

It also means helping leaders develop presence and self-awareness and the discernment skill to know when to step in and when to step back.

This isn’t soft skill fluff. It’s the thing that actually determines how someone makes a call under pressure, or handles conflict without making it worse, or gets a team to actually own their work.

AI is taking over more of the work every year. Which is exactly why Human Intelligence is turning into the real competitive edge.

But none of that matters if it stays theoretical. That’s where the Fractional COO side of what I do comes in. I work with leadership teams and actually help you build the systems and the processes. We dig into who owns what and how they’re held to it.

Insight is nice. Implementation and integration are what change the numbers and drive retention of teams and a great culture. 

What you end up with is a team that’s actually aligned, communication that doesn’t require guesswork, systems simple enough that people actually use them, and leaders who finally have room to focus on what matters instead of putting out fires all day.

Because real growth needs both halves. The internal capacity to lead people well, and the discipline to actually move the organization.

Why We Do What We Do

I have been the overworked business leader, executive and owner who couldn’t see the forest for the trees. I’ve been in your shoes. 

I personally co-built a holistic 8,000 sq. ft. health & fitness center to 1,000 members while raising my daughter as a single mom. I know what it feels like to lie awake at 2 am, wondering how to make payroll, keep my team motivated, and still show up as the leader everyone needed me to be.

I also know what it feels like on the other side when systems run smoothly, the team takes ownership, and I finally have space to breathe and think strategically.

That transformation? It’s what I want for you.

Too many capable business owners are drowning in operations when they should be leading. Too many talented teams are spinning their wheels because no one has given them clear direction and the right tools.

I started Full Pocket Coaching in 2017 to hold space for leaders who are ready to get out of their own way. My mission is to help you identify your blind spots, align your team, and build a business that doesn’t require you to sacrifice your life to run it.

This is the greatest gift of service I can give.

About Marni Blythe

Professional Speaker Fractional COO/CMO/integrator, Best Selling Author

Marni Blythe is a nationally recognized leadership expert, keynote speaker, author, and Fractional Chief Operating Officer who helps healthcare organizations and entrepreneurial leaders build resilient cultures, emotionally intelligent teams, and sustainable operational growth in times of rapid change.

As Founder and CEO of Full Pocket Coaching, Marni partners with independent organizations globally and nationally to strengthen leadership communication, team dynamics, workplace culture, and organizational effectiveness.

With more than two decades of executive leadership experience, Marni is known for translating complex leadership and operational challenges into practical, actionable strategies that drive measurable transformation.

Her work integrates leadership development, emotional intelligence, communication science, mindfulness, operational strategy, and change management to help organizations navigate uncertainty while maintaining strong human connection and team engagement.

Marni began her career in New York City working within the corporate marketing and advertising world, supporting major campaigns and strategic initiatives for Fortune 500 and Fortune 100 companies through leading advertising agencies. This experience gave her a deep understanding of consumer psychology, communication strategy, brand leadership, organizational dynamics, and high-performance team environments. Her corporate foundation continues to shape her ability to help leaders communicate with greater clarity, influence, and emotional intelligence in rapidly evolving industries.

Since then, Marni has served as an integrator, operational strategist, executive coach, and culture consultant for healthcare organizations and privately owned businesses navigating growth, burnout, communication breakdowns, and organizational change.

Today, Marni works extensively with executives, teams, and owners to improve leadership effectiveness, emotional resilience, and organizational culture. Her expertise includes leadership communication, mindful leadership, employee engagement, conflict resolution, change management, operational alignment, and creating psychologically safe workplaces that support both performance and well-being.

As a keynote speaker, Marni is recognized for delivering highly engaging and transformational presentations that combine compelling storytelling with evidence-based leadership principles and practical takeaways. Her signature keynote, Ride the Wave: Leading Human Intelligence in the Age of AI, explores how leaders can preserve trust, emotional intelligence, and authentic human connection amid increasing technological disruption and workplace stress. Through her “Ride the Wave” framework, she equips audiences with concrete tools to navigate uncertainty, regulate reactivity, improve communication, and lead with greater presence and adaptability.

Marni is also a co-author of Culture Catalyst, a leadership and workplace culture resource focused on helping organizations intentionally shape environments where people and performance thrive together. Her work emphasizes that organizational success is not built solely through systems and technology, but through the quality of leadership, communication, and human connection within teams.

Known for her authenticity, relatability, and operational insight, Marni brings a unique blend of executive leadership expertise and emotional intelligence to every stage. Her presentations resonate with healthcare leaders because they address the real-world challenges facing organizations today: burnout, disengagement, communication fatigue, workforce stress, and the growing tension between technological advancement and human-centered leadership.

 

Through her speaking, coaching, and consulting, Marni empowers leaders to create cultures that are more connected, resilient, adaptive, and sustainable in an increasingly complex healthcare landscape.

Speaking Topics

Human Intelligence in an AI World

Summary

  • Identify beliefs driving stress and reactions.

  • Stay grounded during clinical challenges.

  • Lead teams with authenticity and trust.

Target Outcome

Attendees will feel re-energized, more confident in their value, and equipped with practical, human-centered strategies to create deeper connection, stronger momentum, and meaningful growth in a rapidly changing world.

The Formula for Predictable Growth

Summary

  • Focus on the few metrics that matter most.
  • Use data to guide leadership decisions, not punish performance.
  • Translate goals into consistent, predictable execution.

Target Outcome

Empower dental professionals to build emotional resilience, regulate stress, and sustain high performance through mindset mastery — creating a calmer, more cohesive, and patient-centered practice environment.

Culture by Design, Not Default

Summary

  • Establish clear expectations, norms, and accountability across the organization.
  • Align leadership behaviors with the results the organization expects.
  • Build psychological safety while maintaining high performance standards.

Target Outcome

Equip leaders to confidently lead change, build a high-performance culture, and embed continuous innovation into daily operations—resulting in measurable improvements in team engagement, patient satisfaction, and profitability.

Navigating High-Stakes Conversations

Summary

  • Stay grounded and effective under emotional pressure.
  • Address issues directly while preserving trust and respect.
  • Replace avoidance with clarity, care, and productive dialogue.

Target Outcome

Equip healthcare professionals with practical tools to navigate high-stakes, emotionally charged conversations—transforming conflict into connection and clarity.
